# 🌶️ Carrotato Quickstart Guide

Welcome to **Carrotato**! This guide will help you get started with your first chili harvest in just a few minutes.

## 1. Finding Your First Chili
You don't need to craft anything to get started. Explore the world and look for **Wild Chili** plants in the following biomes:
*   **Savannas** & Savanna Plateaus
*   **Jungles** & Sparse Jungles

Wild Chilis appear as fully grown crops. Break them to obtain **Chili** and occasionally **Chili Seeds**.

## 2. Starting a Farm
Once you have seeds, you can start your own chili plantation:
1.  **Prepare Farmland**: Use a hoe on grass or dirt blocks near water.
2.  **Plant**: Right-click the farmland with **Chili Seeds**.
3.  **Wait (or Bone Meal)**: Chilis have 8 growth stages (Age 0 to 7). You can use Bone Meal to speed up the process.
4.  **Harvest**: Once the plant looks large and red (Age 7), break it to receive Chilis and more seeds!

## 3. Culinary Processing
Don't just eat them raw! You can process chilis for better ingredients:
*   **Dry them**: Put a Chili in a **Furnace** or **Smoker** to get a **Dried Chili**.
*   **Grind them**: Place a Dried Chili in any crafting grid to get **Chili Powder**.

## 4. Advanced Spicy Meals
Use your ingredients to craft powerful food items:
*   **Hot Sauce**: Bottle + Chili Powder + Sugar.
*   **Spicy Fried Chicken**: Cooked Chicken + Chili Powder.
*   **Spicy Chili con Carne**: Bowl + Cooked Beef + Raw Chili + Mushroom.

## 5. The Spicy Effect
Eating processed chili foods grants the **Spicy** effect, which provides **Fire Resistance**. Higher-tier meals like Chili con Carne provide longer durations!
